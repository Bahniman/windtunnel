# Windtunnel

Windtunnel is a concept for rehearsing pricing decisions with an explicit model and customer evidence. The public page is a product argument and an illustrative browser sandbox. The current web sandbox uses three hand-set cohort assumptions and 500 seeded Monte Carlo runs; it does not ingest a company's reviews, support history, or transaction data. It is a scenario exercise, not a calibrated forecast or an autonomous launch recommendation.

The intended reader is a product, finance, or business decision-maker weighing a pricing change. State the inputs and model limits beside the outputs. Distinguish the longer-term product concept from the implemented sample, and do not present arbitrary thresholds as validated decision rules.

Core page actions: navigate between argument, sandbox, limits, and sources; switch theme; change price increase, cohort size, and migration strategy; reset the sample; read percentile, downside, churn, and distribution summaries; expand the jargon decoder; follow source and repository links.
