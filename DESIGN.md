# Windtunnel visual direction

Use the shared Riso Poster system from the supplied design guide: cream paper, black ink outlines, blue and pink spot inks, yellow sticker accents, Bricolage Grotesque display, Newsreader narrative emphasis, Space Mono for data, 2.5px ink borders, and hard offset shadows. Use the shared suite header and shared token/style files. Keep halftones in margins, not behind text; keep controls, cohort comparisons, and charts straight and scan-friendly. No glass, blur, gradients, or soft shadows.

The page tells a persuasive product story; its pricing sandbox is an operate-mode sample. Keep assumptions, run count, and model caveats adjacent to outputs. Show the median and tail as percentiles, not as forecast confidence. Use text labels as well as color, keep all inputs and chart summaries keyboard accessible, and preserve reduced-motion support. Avoid a go/no-go recommendation that the illustrative model cannot justify.
